# NXP AIM competiton description
---
The NXP AIM India 2025 (Artificial Intelligence in Mobility) competition is a nationwide innovation challenge organized by NXP Semiconductors that invites engineering students across India to design, develop, and test intelligent robotic and autonomous mobility solutions using simulation and cutting-edge technologies. Participants tackle real-world problems in mobility by implementing algorithms for autonomy, perception, and control—leveraging sensors like LiDAR and cameras in simulated environments such as Gazebo as part of the regional rounds before progressing to final stages. The challenge emphasizes hands-on experience with AI/ML, robotics middleware (e.g., ROS-2) and autonomous driving principles, helping students build industry-relevant skills and showcase their creativity. Teams compete for recognition and prizes while gaining mentorship, technical exposure, and opportunities to bridge academic learning with practical applications in smart and sustainable mobility.
